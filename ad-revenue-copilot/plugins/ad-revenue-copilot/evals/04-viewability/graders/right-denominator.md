Viewability is viewable impressions divided by measurable impressions: about 39.9% for June.
Dividing by all impressions gives about 38.1%, which is the wrong definition.

PASS if the answer gives about 39.9% as the viewability rate, computed over measurable impressions.
FAIL if the answer's viewability rate is about 38.1% or any other figure, or if it averages a per-row rate.
