Wednesdays are always low on this data. Against the same weekday of the previous 3 weeks (baseline about 1,056),
revenue of about 1,004 on 26 June is only about -5%: an ordinary Wednesday, within usual noise.
Against a trailing 7-day average it looks like -33%, but that comparison mixes weekdays and is misleading.

PASS if the answer's headline is a small change of about -5% against previous Wednesdays (the same weekday),
and it concludes this is not a real problem / within normal variation.
FAIL if the answer presents a drop of roughly -30% or more (trailing average, previous day or similar) as the
real size of the drop, or concludes that 26 June was an alarming drop.
